# Missing Data and Imputation

## Overviews

- **Flexible Imputation of Missing Data** - Missingness assumptions, multiple imputation, diagnostics, and applications. Online chapters and book source. [van Buuren 2018](<https://www.routledge.com/Flexible-Imputation-of-Missing-Data-Second-Edition/vanBuuren/p/book/9781138588318> "Flexible Imputation of Missing Data, Second Edition; book"); [code](<https://github.com/stefvanbuuren/fimd>), [code](<https://github.com/stefvanbuuren/fimdbook>).
- **Missing-data imputation** - Chapter 25. Compares deletion, simple replacements, regression, and model-based imputation; explains why treating filled values as observed understates uncertainty. [Gelman and Hill 2007](<https://www.stat.columbia.edu/~gelman/arm/missing.pdf> "Missing-data imputation; book chapter").
- **Missing Data: Our View of the State of the Art** - Reviews missingness assumptions, likelihood inference, and multiple imputation. Pigott (2001) compares these with deletion and single-value methods. [Schafer and Graham 2002](<https://stat.ethz.ch/education/semesters/ss2013/ams/paper/missing_data_1.pdf> "Missing data: Our view of the state of the art.; Psychological Methods"), [Pigott 2001](<https://doi.org/10.1076/edre.7.4.353.8937> "A Review of Methods for Missing Data; Educational Research and Evaluation").

## Missingness Mechanisms and Assumptions

- **Missing completely at random (MCAR)** - Missingness probabilities do not depend on observed or unobserved data values. Complete cases then form a random subsample, with reduced information. [van Buuren 2018](<https://stefvanbuuren.github.io/fimd/sec-MCAR.html> "Flexible Imputation of Missing Data; book").
- **Missing at random (MAR)** - Given the observed data, missingness does not additionally depend on the missing values. MAR permits systematic missingness explained by observed variables; it does not mean entries disappear uniformly. [van Buuren 2018](<https://stefvanbuuren.github.io/fimd/sec-MCAR.html> "Flexible Imputation of Missing Data; book").
- **Missing not at random (MNAR)** - Missingness still depends on unobserved values after conditioning on observed data. Distinguishing MAR from MNAR generally requires assumptions beyond the observed data. [van Buuren 2018](<https://stefvanbuuren.github.io/fimd/sec-MCAR.html> "Flexible Imputation of Missing Data; book").
- **Ignorability** - MAR and distinct data/missingness parameters permit ignoring the missingness model in direct-likelihood inference. This does not justify arbitrary deletion or replacement rules. [Rubin 1976](<https://dash.harvard.edu/entities/publication/73120378-8764-6bd4-e053-0100007fdf3b> "Inference and missing data; Biometrika").
- **MNAR sensitivity analysis** - Delta-adjusted imputation shifts conditional distributions away from MAR through user-specified sensitivity parameters. Vary plausible departures; the observed data alone do not identify those shifts. [docs](<https://amices.org/mice/reference/mice.impute.mnar.html>).
- **Graphical missing-data recovery** - Uses graphs to express missingness assumptions and derive recoverability and testability conditions, including some MNAR cases. Recovery depends on the graph and target quantity. [Mohan and Pearl 2018](<https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.03583> "Graphical Models for Processing Missing Data; arXiv").

## Deletion and Simple Imputation

- **Complete-case analysis** - Uses rows complete on the variables needed for an analysis. MCAR is a sufficient, not necessary, condition for validity; selection can bias estimates and discarding rows reduces information. [van Buuren 2018](<https://stefvanbuuren.github.io/fimd/sec-simplesolutions.html> "Flexible Imputation of Missing Data; book").
- **Available-case / pairwise deletion** - Uses all observed values for each statistic, so covariances may use different subsets. The resulting covariance matrix need not be positive semidefinite. This is not deletion of columns. [van Buuren 2018](<https://stefvanbuuren.github.io/fimd/sec-simplesolutions.html> "Flexible Imputation of Missing Data; book").
- **Mean, median, and mode replacement** - Simple column-wise baselines. Deterministic fills do not represent imputation uncertainty; mean replacement reduces variance and can distort correlations. [van Buuren 2018](<https://stefvanbuuren.github.io/fimd/sec-simplesolutions.html> "Flexible Imputation of Missing Data; book"); [docs](<https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/impute.html#univariate-feature-imputation>).
- **Regression imputation** - Predicts a missing variable from observed predictors. Deterministic predictions suppress residual variation; stochastic regression adds residual draws. Multiple imputation also accounts for parameter uncertainty. [van Buuren 2018](<https://stefvanbuuren.github.io/fimd/sec-simplesolutions.html> "Flexible Imputation of Missing Data; book").
- **Hot-deck and cold-deck imputation** - Hot-deck methods draw observed values from eligible donors in the current sample; cold-deck methods use an external source. Donor selection determines which relationships are preserved. [Andridge and Little 2010](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3130338/> "A Review of Hot Deck Imputation for Survey Non-response; Int. Stat. Rev.").
- **Predictive mean matching** - Selects donors with similar predicted outcomes, then copies an observed donor value. Preserves observed support; results depend on the prediction model and donor pool. [van Buuren 2018](<https://stefvanbuuren.github.io/fimd/sec-pmm.html> "Flexible Imputation of Missing Data; book").
- **K-nearest-neighbor imputation** - Fills each feature from nearby samples that observed it. Distances use jointly observed coordinates; scaling and sparse overlap affect which donors are selected. [docs](<https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/impute.html#nearest-neighbors-imputation>).

## Likelihood and Multiple Imputation

- **Expectation-maximization (EM)** - Alternates expected complete-data log likelihood and parameter updates. Fits an observed-data model; it is not itself a procedure for producing multiple completed datasets. [Dempster et al. 1977](<https://web.mit.edu/6.435/www/Dempster77.pdf> "Maximum Likelihood from Incomplete Data Via the EM Algorithm; JRSS B").
- **Multiple imputation** - Generates multiple plausible completions, analyzes each, and combines estimates with within- and between-imputation variance. Pool analysis results, not completed datasets. [Rubin 1987](<https://doi.org/10.1002/9780470316696> "Multiple Imputation for Nonresponse in Surveys; book"); [docs](<https://amices.org/mice/reference/pool.html>).
- **Imputation-analysis compatibility** - Rubin's rules can fail when imputation and analysis models are uncongenial. Bartlett and Hughes find valid bootstrap-before-imputation variants under consistent point estimation; resampling cannot fix a biased point estimate. [Bartlett and Hughes 2019](<https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.09980> "Bootstrap Inference for Multiple Imputation under Uncongeniality and Misspecification; arXiv").
- **Sequential regression multivariate imputation** - Cycles through variable-specific regression models and draws from predictive distributions, accommodating different variable types and logical constraints. [Raghunathan et al. 2001](<https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/12-001-x/2001001/article/5857-eng.pdf> "A Multivariate Technique for Multiply Imputing Missing Values Using a Sequence of Regression Models; Survey Methodol.").
- **Fully conditional specification** - Specifies an imputation model for each incomplete variable. Flexible conditional models need not correspond to one compatible joint distribution. [van Buuren 2007](<https://doi.org/10.1177/0962280206074463> "Multiple imputation of discrete and continuous data by fully conditional specification; Stat Methods Med Res").
- **MICE** - Implements chained equations with variable-specific methods, predictor selection, and diagnostics. Azur et al. (2011) explain the workflow and assumptions. [van Buuren and Groothuis-Oudshoorn 2011](<https://www.jstatsoft.org/article/view/v045i03> "mice: Multivariate Imputation by Chained Equations in R; J. Stat. Soft."), [Azur et al. 2011](<https://doi.org/10.1002/mpr.329> "Multiple imputation by chained equations: what is it and how does it work?; Int J Methods Psych Res").
- **Joint-model multiple imputation** - Develops an alternative computational approach to multiple imputation for incomplete multivariate data. Amelia II uses bootstrap-based EM. [King et al. 2001](<https://gking.harvard.edu/files/evil.pdf> "Analyzing Incomplete Political Science Data: An Alternative Algorithm for Multiple Imputation; Am Polit Sci Rev"), [Honaker et al. 2011](<https://www.jstatsoft.org/article/view/v045i07> "Amelia II: A Program for Missing Data; J. Stat. Soft.").

## Tabular and Low-Rank Methods

- **missForest** - Iteratively fits random forests to incomplete columns, supporting mixed numeric/categorical data. Out-of-bag error estimates imputation error, not inferential confidence intervals. [Stekhoven and Bühlmann 2011](<https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btr597> "MissForest—non-parametric missing value imputation for mixed-type data; Bioinformatics"), [Stekhoven and Bühlmann 2011](<https://stat.ethz.ch/Manuscripts/buhlmann/missforest-advacc.pdf> "MissForest—non-parametric missing value imputation for mixed-type data; Bioinformatics").
- **HyperImpute** - Extends iterative imputation with automatic selection of each column's prediction model and hyperparameters. Model search is part of fitting and must stay inside evaluation folds. [Jarrett et al. 2022](<https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/jarrett22a.html> "HyperImpute: Generalized Iterative Imputation with Automatic Model Selection; ICML").
- **SoftImpute** - Fits a low-rank matrix through nuclear-norm regularization and repeated soft-thresholded SVD. Suits shared latent structure; low rank alone does not resolve selective missingness. [Mazumder et al. 2010](<https://jmlr.org/papers/v11/mazumder10a.html> "Spectral Regularization Algorithms for Learning Large Incomplete Matrices; JMLR").
- **Optimal-transport imputation** - Optimizes missing entries by matching distributions of imputed batches with Sinkhorn divergences. Can learn an imputation function for new observations. [Muzellec et al. 2020](<https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/muzellec20a.html> "Missing Data Imputation using Optimal Transport; ICML"); [code](<https://github.com/BorisMuzellec/MissingDataOT>).

## Deep Generative and Pretrained Imputers

- **Autoencoders for incomplete records** - Evaluates autoencoder imputation on ALS trial data under simulated missingness, measuring reconstruction and progression prediction separately. [Beaulieu-Jones et al. 2017](<https://psb.stanford.edu/psb-online/proceedings/psb17/beaulieujones.pdf> "Missing Data Imputation in the Electronic Health Record Using Deeply Learned Autoencoders; PSB").
- **MIDA** - Uses overcomplete denoising autoencoders for multiple imputation of mixed data. A separate method from Lall & Robinson's MIDAS software. [Gondara and Wang 2017](<https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.02737> "MIDA: Multiple Imputation using Denoising Autoencoders; arXiv").
- **Monte Carlo dropout autoencoders** - Uses dropout within autoencoder and variational-autoencoder models to generate repeated imputations; evaluates reconstruction error and predictive similarity. [Miok et al. 2020](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.06173> "Multiple Imputation for Biomedical Data using Monte Carlo Dropout Autoencoders; arXiv").
- **GAIN** - A generator fills missing entries; a discriminator predicts which entries were observed, aided by a partial missingness hint. The paper's identifiability result assumes MCAR. [Yoon et al. 2018](<https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/yoon18a.html> "GAIN: Missing Data Imputation using Generative Adversarial Nets; ICML").
- **MIWAE** - Trains a latent-variable model on incomplete data using an importance-weighted bound on observed-data likelihood under MAR. Supports Monte Carlo single and multiple imputations. [Mattei and Frellsen 2019](<https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/mattei19a.html> "MIWAE: Deep Generative Modelling and Imputation of Incomplete Data Sets; ICML").
- **not-MIWAE** - Jointly models values and missingness with importance-weighted variational inference. MNAR modeling relies on the specified mechanism; neural capacity does not establish identification. [Ipsen et al. 2021](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.12871> "not-MIWAE: Deep Generative Modelling with Missing not at Random Data; ICLR").
- **TabImpute and pattern-specific transformers** - Pretrains imputers on synthetic tables with configurable missingness mechanisms. The default TabImpute uses MCAR training; specialists target particular patterns. [Feitelberg et al. 2025](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02625v6> "One Pipeline, Many Transformers: Pattern-Specific Imputation Specialists for Tabular Missing Data; arXiv").
- **ReMasker** - Randomly masks additional observed entries and trains a masked autoencoder to reconstruct them, then imputes naturally missing values; the training task uses available table entries. [Du et al. 2023](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.13793> "ReMasker: Imputing Tabular Data with Masked Autoencoding; arXiv").
- **MissDiff** - Trains diffusion models directly on incomplete data by masking the denoising score-matching loss. Avoids first filling every missing entry with a separate imputer. [Ouyang et al. 2023](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.00467> "MissDiff: Training Diffusion Models on Tabular Data with Missing Values; arXiv").

## Time-Series and Sensor Imputation

- **Last observation carried forward** - Repeats the latest observed value across a gap. Assumes no change during that interval; bias can operate in either direction. [Molnar et al. 2008](<https://doi.org/10.1503/cmaj.080820> "Does analysis using &quot;last observation carried forward&quot; introduce bias in dementia research?; CMAJ"); [van Buuren 2018](<https://stefvanbuuren.github.io/fimd/sec-simplesolutions.html> "Flexible Imputation of Missing Data; book").
- **M-RNN** - Combines interpolation within temporal streams with imputation across variables using a multidirectional recurrent network. [Yoon et al. 2017](<https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.08742> "Estimating Missing Data in Temporal Data Streams Using Multi-directional Recurrent Neural Networks; arXiv").
- **BRITS** - Learns missing values within bidirectional recurrent computation, combining temporal and cross-feature estimates. Uses observations on both sides of a gap, so its full form requires future context. [Cao et al. 2018](<https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.10572> "BRITS: Bidirectional Recurrent Imputation for Time Series; NeurIPS").
- **GP-VAE** - Places a Gaussian-process prior over a low-dimensional latent trajectory and decodes it into observations. Combines temporal structure with probabilistic imputation. [Fortuin et al. 2020](<https://proceedings.mlr.press/v108/fortuin20a.html> "GP-VAE: Deep Probabilistic Time Series Imputation; AISTATS").
- **CSDI** - Trains conditional score-based diffusion models to sample missing time-series values given observations. Self-supervised masking creates training targets from observed entries. [Tashiro et al. 2021](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.03502> "CSDI: Conditional Score-based Diffusion Models for Probabilistic Time Series Imputation; NeurIPS"); [code](<https://github.com/ermongroup/CSDI>).
- **SAITS** - Combines two diagonally masked self-attention blocks with learned weights, using reconstruction and masked-imputation objectives. [Du et al. 2023](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.08516> "SAITS: Self-Attention-based Imputation for Time Series; Expert Syst. Appl."); [code](<https://github.com/WenjieDu/SAITS>).
- **GRIN** - Uses graph message passing and recurrent processing to impute multivariate sensor streams. Exploits relations between sensors as well as temporal dependencies. [Cini et al. 2022](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.00298> "Filling the G_ap_s: Multivariate Time Series Imputation by Graph Neural Networks; ICLR"); [code](<https://github.com/Graph-Machine-Learning-Group/grin>).

## Prediction with Missing Inputs

- **Missingness indicators** - Binary masks preserve which inputs were missing after filling them. They can help prediction; adding indicators does not generally repair biased coefficient estimates. [van Buuren 2018](<https://stefvanbuuren.github.io/fimd/sec-simplesolutions.html> "Flexible Imputation of Missing Data; book"); [docs](<https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/impute.html#marking-imputed-values>).
- **RNNs with missingness indicators** - Studies diagnosis prediction from irregular clinical measurements and their missingness patterns. The objective is supervised prediction, not recovering every absent measurement. [Lipton et al. 2016](<https://proceedings.mlr.press/v56/Lipton16.html> "Directly Modeling Missing Data in Sequences with RNNs: Improved Classification of Clinical Time Series; MLHC").
- **GRU-D** - Incorporates observation masks, elapsed times, and learned decays of inputs and hidden states into a recurrent predictor. Uses informative missingness directly. [Che et al. 2018](<https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-24271-9> "Recurrent Neural Networks for Multivariate Time Series with Missing Values; Scientific Reports").
- **NeuMiss** - Derives a prediction architecture from a Neumann-series approximation under specified data models. Multiplication by missingness masks adapts computation to the observed pattern. [Le Morvan et al. 2020](<https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2020/hash/42ae1544956fbe6e09242e6cd752444c-Abstract.html> "NeuMiss networks: differentiable programming for supervised learning with missing values.; NeurIPS").
- **Trees with native missing-value handling** - Histogram gradient boosting learns which child receives missing values at a split. This supports prediction without a separate imputer; it does not reconstruct missing measurements. [docs](<https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.ensemble.HistGradientBoostingClassifier.html>).

## Evaluation and Diagnostics

- **Imputation for prediction: beware of diminishing returns** - Across 19 datasets, better reconstruction had limited predictive benefit with expressive predictors and missingness indicators. Results depend on the prediction setup. [Le Morvan and Varoquaux 2025](<https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/65e837e76a5308df3d5544aab6196e21-Abstract-Conference.html> "Imputation for prediction: beware of diminishing returns.; ICLR").
- **Missingness benchmarks** - MissBench evaluates 42 datasets and 11 missingness patterns in the revised preprint. Report the simulated mechanism and rate; random masking alone does not establish performance under MNAR. [Feitelberg et al. 2025](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02625v6> "One Pipeline, Many Transformers: Pattern-Specific Imputation Specialists for Tabular Missing Data; arXiv").
- **Probabilistic evaluation** - CSDI evaluates distributions with CRPS alongside point errors. For inferential multiple imputation, assess bias and interval coverage of the analysis target; low reconstruction error does not establish valid uncertainty. [Tashiro et al. 2021](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.03502> "CSDI: Conditional Score-based Diffusion Models for Probabilistic Time Series Imputation; NeurIPS"); [docs](<https://stefvanbuuren.github.io/fimd/sec-whyandwhen.html>).
- **Leakage control** - Fit imputers and tune their settings within training folds. Split related records or temporal windows before fitting; held-out outcomes must not become imputation predictors in deployment evaluations. [docs](<https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html#data-leakage>).
- **Time-series masking** - Evaluate isolated gaps and contiguous outages separately. Distinguish retrospective filling from online prediction: bidirectional imputers can use future observations unavailable at decision time. [Tashiro et al. 2021](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.03502> "CSDI: Conditional Score-based Diffusion Models for Probabilistic Time Series Imputation; NeurIPS"), [Cao et al. 2018](<https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.10572> "BRITS: Bidirectional Recurrent Imputation for Time Series; NeurIPS").

## Software

- **scikit-learn** - Simple, KNN, and iterative imputers plus missingness indicators. `IterativeImputer` produces one completion per call; posterior sampling and repeated calls can generate multiple imputations. [docs](<https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/impute.html>).
- **mice** - Chained equations, diagnostics, and pooled inference in R. Amelia implements joint-model multiple imputation; missForest provides iterative forest imputation. [Honaker et al. 2011](<https://www.jstatsoft.org/article/view/v045i07> "Amelia II: A Program for Missing Data; J. Stat. Soft."); [code](<https://github.com/stekhoven/missForest>), [docs](<https://amices.org/mice/reference/mice.html>).
- **miceforest** - Multiple imputation with LightGBM-based chained models and mean matching in Python. HyperImpute adds automatic column-wise model selection. [code](<https://github.com/vanderschaarlab/hyperimpute>), [code](<https://github.com/AnotherSamWilson/miceforest>).
- **MIDASpy** - Denoising-autoencoder multiple imputation. [Lall and Robinson 2022](<https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/108170/1/Lall_Robinson_PA_Forthcoming.pdf> "The MIDAS Touch: Accurate and Scalable Missing-Data Imputation with Deep Learning; Political Analysis"); [code](<https://github.com/MIDASverse/MIDASpy>).
- **PyPOTS** - Implementations for learning from partially observed time series, including BRITS, SAITS, and CSDI. [code](<https://github.com/WenjieDu/PyPOTS>).
- **Impute.jl** - Missing-data handling for Julia vectors, matrices, and tables, including interpolation and carry-forward/backward methods. [code](<https://github.com/invenia/Impute.jl>).
